# Operator prompts (for the later OpenAI agent)

## Launch the investigation

> Audit April 2026 SaaS subscription billing against the signed contractual terms for every customer. Look for underbilling, overbilling, duplicate invoices, missed effective-date changes and anomalies shared by a pricing template. Cite the contract, amendment and invoice for each finding. Do not send, modify or approve invoices. Pause for my approval before any financial action.

## Challenge / theatrical twist

> Before proposing actions, actively look for signed amendments or expiry dates that could invalidate your top findings. If you find evidence that contradicts an earlier hypothesis, retract it and show me why. Then investigate whether any remaining issues share a common root cause across accounts.

## Human checkpoint

> Show the cases where the documentation is contradictory or evidence is missing. Present the exact conflicting terms and your recommended human verification steps. Do not choose one contract term without authorization.

## Business value

> Split potential additional receivables, potential customer credits, and duplicate-charge exposure. Do not count them all as savings. Estimate analyst hours saved only if we provide a defensible baseline; clearly label all assumptions.
